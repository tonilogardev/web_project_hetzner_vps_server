Cookie Policy

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are stored on your device (computer or mobile device) when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners to make their sites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

What cookies do we use?

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: They are necessary for the basic functioning of the site. These cookies enable functionalities such as user authentication and security. Without these cookies, you could not log into your account.
  • Authentication cookies: We use Auth0 cookies to manage the login and authentication process, which allows us to offer you a secure experience.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We use Auth0 as our authentication provider, which uses cookies to manage your session securely.

How can I control cookies?

You can decide whether to accept or reject cookies. When you visit our site for the first time, we present you with a cookie banner that allows you to choose. If you reject essential cookies, some parts of the site may not work correctly, such as login and authentication.

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Reconsider cookies" button that appears when you have previously rejected cookies.

How long will cookies remain on my device?

The time a cookie will remain on your device depends on whether it is a "persistent" or "session" cookie. Session cookies will remain on your device until you close your browser. Persistent cookies will remain on your device until they expire or are deleted.

Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect, for example, changes to the cookies we use or for other operational, legal or regulatory reasons. Therefore, please review this Cookie Policy regularly to stay informed about our use of cookies and related technologies.
